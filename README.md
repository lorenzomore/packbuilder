# Packbuilder

Keep track of your gear, and build out what each person is carrying
before a trip.

The app has two tabs: **Gear library** and **People & packs**.

## Gear library

Your master list of gear — everything you own or might pack, in one
place.

- **Add item** — give it a name, a category, an optional season, and
  a note if you want one.
- **Season** marks something as Summer-only or Winter-only, like a
  cap or a beanie. Leave it blank for anything year-round, like a
  water bottle.
- Use the **search box** and **category filter** to find things once
  the list grows.
- Click the pencil or trash icon on a row to **edit or delete** it.
- **Upload** lets you add a lot of gear at once from a spreadsheet
  (`.csv` or `.xlsx`).
- **Template** downloads a ready-to-fill spreadsheet with dropdowns
  built in — including a second sheet where you can add your own
  categories.
- **Clear all** empties the whole library. It asks you to confirm
  first.

## People & packs

This is where gear gets assigned to a person.

- **Add person**, then **Add pack** for each bag they're carrying
  (day pack, overnight pack, whatever you need).
- **Add gear** opens your library so you can tick off everything
  that pack needs, grouped by category. There's a Summer / Winter
  filter to narrow it down.
- **Add custom item** is for anything that doesn't belong in your
  permanent library — a one-off or borrowed item.
- Each item in a pack has a **checkbox** (mark it packed) and its own
  **note** — editing that note only affects this pack, not the
  library.

## Sharing a pack list

- **Copy all** / **Copy to clipboard** copies everything (or just one
  person's packs) as plain text, ready to paste into WhatsApp or any
  messaging app.
- **PDF export** and **Markdown export** save a file instead — the
  Markdown version is built to drop straight into Obsidian.

## Where your data lives

Everything is saved in your browser, not on a server. It'll be there
next time you open the app in the same browser, but it won't follow
you to another browser or device.
